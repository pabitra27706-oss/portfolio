The Learning Archive

This archive has moved into the learning engine.

It used to be a separate repository with its own site, and publishing to it meant cloning, copying files into three folders, editing an index by hand and pushing — every time you finished a task. On a phone, that is a lot of remembering, and it is exactly the kind of thing that quietly stops happening.

It is now part of the Study-with-ai repository, and it is generated from the learning engine on every build. Your code, the verdict, the score, your notes and the misconceptions are all in the same entry. It cannot fall behind the work, because it is made of the work.
